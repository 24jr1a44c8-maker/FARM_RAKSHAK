Deep Technical Architecture

Hybrid CNN + Qiskit VQC Pipeline

Detailed algorithmic stages of the precision crop inspection system

1
Live Video Frame Acquisition getUserMedia
Real-time camera feed sampled at 2.5s serialized intervals across designated farm zones.
↓
2
Plant-Region Filter & Proposals HSV + MobileNetV2
Green-hue segmentation combined with ImageNet foliage screening to locate candidate crop leaves and reject non-plant regions.
↓
3
Deep Visual Feature Extraction 1280-dim Vector
Pretrained MobileNetV2 backbone extracts rich visual representations of leaf texture, lesions, discolorations, and pest damage.
↓
4
Feature Scaling & PCA Compression StandardScaler + PCA(n=4)
Principal Component Analysis compresses the 1280 visual features into 4 orthogonal components suitable for quantum register encoding.
↓
5
Quantum Feature Angle Encoding Qiskit Ry(θ) Gates
Each of the 4 reduced features is encoded into rotation angles around the Y-axis across 4 qubits: |ψ⟩ = ⊗ Ry(θᵢ)|0⟩.
↓
6
Variational Quantum Classifier (VQC) RealAmplitudes + COBYLA
Parameterized ansatz with alternating Ry rotations and entangling CNOT ladders executed on Qiskit's StatevectorSampler simulator.
↓
7
Probability Classification & Zone Aggregation Healthy / Pest / Disease
Multi-class probability distribution evaluated against confidence threshold (0.55). Confident detections are aggregated into zone risk metrics.
