🌾 Farmer Guide: How to Use

1

Select a Farm Zone

Choose which sector of your field you are currently inspecting (e.g. Zone 1 North-East Plot).

2

Start the Camera

Allow browser camera access and point your mobile or laptop camera steadily at crop leaves.

3

Scan Plants in Real Time

Press "Start Continuous Scan". The system samples crop frames every 2.5 seconds.

4

AI & Quantum Detection

MobileNetV2 and Qiskit VQC evaluate healthy foliage vs pest and disease symptoms with bounding box highlights.

5

Results Aggregated to Selected Zone

Confident detections update the zone's healthy count and affected percentage.

6

Farm Map Shows Risk Heatmap

The 6-zone farm grid updates from green to amber or red as high infection rates are logged.

7

Review Priority Action Queue

Inspect high-risk zones first to prevent crop loss, saving labor and targeted treatment costs.
